How to play

  • Roll a 6 to bring a token out of base.
  • Race all 4 tokens around the board and into your home lane. You need the exact roll to reach home.
  • Land on an enemy to capture it and send it back to base. Stars and start squares are safe.
  • A 6, a capture or reaching home earns another roll. Three 6s in a row ends your turn.
  • Pass over a glowing orb to collect a power-up (you can hold 2). Use them before you roll by tapping them in your panel.
