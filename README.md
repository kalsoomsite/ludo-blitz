# Ludo Blitz

Classic 4-player Ludo, supercharged with power-ups. Play against bots or pass-and-play with friends on one device. Installable as an app (PWA) and works offline.

**Play:** https://kalsoomsite.github.io/ludo-blitz/

## Power-ups
Collect glowing orbs on the track (hold up to 2) and use them before you roll:

| Power-up | Effect |
|---|---|
| Shield | Protects one of your tokens from capture for 2 rounds |
| Turbo | Your next move travels double distance |
| Lucky Die | Choose exactly what you roll |
| Strike | Sends any exposed enemy token back to base |
| Freeze | The next player skips their turn |
| Swap | Trade places with an enemy token on the track |

## Rules
- Roll a 6 to leave base. Exact roll needed to reach home.
- Land on an enemy to capture it. Stars and start squares are safe.
- A 6, a capture or reaching home gives another roll. Three 6s in a row ends your turn.
